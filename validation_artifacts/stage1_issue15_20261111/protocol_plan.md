## Exact yaw definitions and decomposition
Original yaw error = sum valid abs(root_ang_vel_b_z-command_z)/sum valid, rad/s. xy error is original body XY speed L2. Full20s nonterminal post-step samples include ordinary episodes after failure reset, matching13/14. Also show initial-episode prefix sensitivity; terminal samples excluded.
Per-trial mean signed body-z yaw-rate bias, absolute/rms trial bias (avoid opposite curves canceling), temporal population SD(ddof0). With trial counts c, sums s, squares q, total yaw MSE numerator = sum(q-2*target*s+target^2*c); bias energy=sum c*(s/c-target)^2; within energy=sum c*(q/c-(s/c)^2). Identity checked. Oscillation share=within/(within+bias), first computed per job then average3eval seeds within training seed. >0.5 = oscillation dominant; <0.5 = persistent curve dominant. If multi-training-seed95% includes0.5, classify not clearly dominant. Single-training-seed labels descriptive only, no fake training CI.
Euler heading computed from wxyz quaternion atan2(2(wz+xy),1-2(y^2+z^2)). Its wrapped per-step difference accumulated over valid steps; reset jumps excluded. Body-z angular velocity is not generally Euler heading derivative under tilt. Save starting/ending yaw, cumulative signed heading per trial for all nonterminal episodes and initial prefix. Full20s heading drift (signed mean, mean abs, median abs,p95 abs, degrees) only for no-reset survivors, with original N/falls/survivor counts and explicit selection/censoring limitation.
