Improvement is substantial, but the symmetric cohort mean is 48.044 degrees, above the predeclared 30 degree threshold. Tracking recovery passes: XY error ratios versus control_int are 0.9724/0.8787 at 0.5/0.8m/s; speed ratios are 0.9874/1.0095. Positive/negative yaw-command error ratios are 0.9514/0.9696. Overall robustness acceptance fails.

At 0.8m/s the 5-20s signed heading slope is 1.510 deg/s [0.433, 2.956] and complete-cycle half-cycle sum is 0.551 degrees [0.133, 1.126]. The sole unimplemented next recommendation is the original/reflected policy by original/strict symmetric USD 2x2 diagnostic. This study does not establish the physical cause. No extra diagnostic experiments were run.
